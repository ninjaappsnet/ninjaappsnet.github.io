How Diagrove works

Diagrove draws precise diagrams on your Mac and iPad, and then makes them do something. Each page of the guide takes one part of the app and says what it’s for and how to use it, with the differences between the Mac and the iPad noted where they come up. If you’d rather learn by building something, start with the tutorials; for one task at a time, the how-to pages; and for questions about the app itself, the support page.

01

Getting started with Diagrove

What you need, the ways to start a diagram on the Mac and the iPad, the parts of a Diagrove window, and how saving and undo work.

 02

Drawing

Shapes, lines that stay connected, text, colours, snapping and smart guides, rulers, groups, tables, pictures, masks, layers and canvases.

 03

Outline and auto-layout

Edit a diagram as a tree of topics in the Outline, and let auto-layout and Tidy put its shapes in order as a flow or a tree.

 04

Stencils and tints

Find one of 1,364 built-in stencils, place it, recolour every face with one tint, and keep stencils of your own in libraries you can share.

 05

Isometric canvases

Draw in isometric projection: a grid on three axes, a floor and two walls, shapes with height, stacking by elevation and stencils that stand up.

 06

Templates

Start from one of 63 templates in ten categories, from flowcharts and roadmaps to isometric offices, on White or Ink paper, then make it yours.

 07

The prompt bar

Draw a diagram from a line of steps or a description, change one in plain words, and see every change on the canvas before you apply it.

 08

Play mode and actions

Give a graphic an action, such as going to another canvas, opening a link or switching a variable, then click or tap through the diagram in Play mode.

 09

Bindings and live data

Bind a graphic’s text, colour, visibility or progress to a variable, a JSON file, a web link or a shortcut’s output, and see the values live.

 10

Import and export

Export PNG, PDF, SVG or a web player, automate with Shortcuts and links, and bring in SVG drawings, OmniGraffle files, sketches, Diagrove text and Mermaid.

 11

The command-line tool

Install the diagrove command-line tool, make, check, render and change documents from Terminal, and connect its MCP server to an AI assistant.

 12

Diagrove and Dayfolio

What works between Diagrove and Dayfolio today: Send to Dayfolio, Shortcuts and links.

 13

Diagrove on iPad

Draw with touch, Apple Pencil, a trackpad or a keyboard, sketch a diagram by hand, keep documents in Files and iCloud Drive, and see what is Mac only.
