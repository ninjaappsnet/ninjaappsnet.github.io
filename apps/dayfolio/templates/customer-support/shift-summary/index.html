Its fields

Add it to a journal

  1. Open the template library: on a Mac, choose View › Template Library (⇧⌘L); on iPhone and iPad, go to More › Templates.
  2. Choose the Customer Support pack, then Shift summary.
  3. Choose Add to and the journal, or Customize… to change its fields first.

Templates from the library are free to add and to change. To start a whole journal with this template and its neighbours, see the Customer Support pack. More about templates and fields in Templates and fields.
