型 TEMPLATES — DAYFOLIO

Templates made for your work

Dayfolio comes with a library of 75 templates in 16 packs, one pack for each kind of work. A template is the shape of an entry: values such as a status or a duration at the top, and the writing below them.

In the app, you add a template to a journal from the template library, or start a journal with a whole pack. Templates from the library are free to add and to change. Each page here shows a template’s fields, with their options and prompts, and an example entry filled in the way the app draws it. The guide explains templates and fields in the app.
