Its fields

Values sit at the top of an entry and the writing fields below them, full width. A field’s prompt is the hint you see while it’s empty.

  1. Team moodMood

  2. TagsTags

  3. SprintText

    e.g. S-39

  4. Went wellChecklist

    What worked this sprint?

  5. Didn’t go wellChecklist

    What got in the way?

  6. Try next sprintChecklist

    What’s the one experiment worth trying?

    Carries over: a new entry starts with the items you haven’t ticked in your last entry with this template.

Add it to a journal

  1. Open the template library: on a Mac, choose View › Template Library (⇧⌘L); on iPhone and iPad, go to More › Templates.
  2. Choose the Software Engineering pack, then Sprint retro.
  3. Choose Add to and the journal, or Customize… to change its fields first.

Templates from the library are free to add and to change. To start a whole journal with this template and its neighbours, see the Software Engineering pack. More about templates and fields in Templates and fields.
