How FlectoBI works

FlectoBI keeps two jobs in one Workbook: a Flow that shapes your data, Step by Step, and the Reports that chart what it makes. Each page of the guide takes one part of the app and says what it’s for and how to use it. If you’d rather learn by building something, start with the tutorials; questions about the app itself are answered on the support page.
