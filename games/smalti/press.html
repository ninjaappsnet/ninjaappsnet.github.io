About Smalti

Smalti is a zen restoration game about bringing handcrafted ceramic mosaics back to life. You draw glazed pieces from your tray, turn them until they seat, and when the last piece lands the board stops being a puzzle: tiles settle in a ripple, grout fuses, a glaze light-sweep crosses the finished artwork, and the game holds the shot so you can look at what you restored. Built by one developer in native SwiftUI with no game engine, it plays entirely offline and never breaks the calm — no ads at all, ever, no energy bars, no clock chasing you during play.

Feature highlights

  • The restoration moment — every solved board fuses into a held, keepable artwork; completed mosaics fill a personal gallery.
  • One daily mosaic, same for everyone — deterministic generation means the whole world restores the same board; streaks with earnable freezes, a Monthly Mosaic that grows from your solved days, spoiler-free share cards, and Game Center leaderboards that reset with the content.
  • The Fold — mid-campaign, chiral pairs deal together and lock into mirror symmetry; a signature mechanic taught by a visible shimmer tell, marked by shape and luminance, never color alone.
  • Fully offline — the entire game works in airplane mode; no account, no login, no server.
  • Player-respecting by design — hidden clock during play, hints that never dead-end, an honest skip for stuck players, no engineered fail states, no ads at all, ever; color-blind assist, Reduce Motion, Dynamic Type, a left-handed layout and a full dark theme all ship in settings.
  • 395 handcrafted-feeling levels across 9 packs, a Masterpiece Commission closing each pack, Atelier Endless with a Game Center depth board, endless relax — with 51 journey levels free, the free tier is a real game, not a demo.

History

Smalti is the work of a single developer who set out to answer one question: can a puzzle game feel premium, calm, and genuinely native without a game engine? The answer became a pure-SwiftUI build with no game engine and every line of gameplay code first-party (the one licensed third-party asset is the music theme), a deterministic level generator that lets the entire world share one daily mosaic, and a comfort bar — color-blind assist, Reduce Motion, Dynamic Type, a left-handed layout, a full dark theme — held to the same standard as the art. Development ran through 2026 as a nights-and-weekends craft project that grew a 395-level journey, and the game ships in 2026 with the same rule it started with: nothing in it is allowed to break the calm — no ads at all, ever, no energy bars, no clock.
