助 SUPPORT — SMALTI

Something wrong?

Write to support@ninjaapps.net

A real person reads it, and you will hear back within 24–48 hours. Your iOS version, your iPhone model and a screenshot will get you a better answer faster.

I paid for the unlock, and it is gone

Open the paywall from the crown on the main menu and tap Restore Purchases. One-time unlocks restore free on any device signed into the same Apple Account, so this covers a new phone, a reinstall, or a second device. The unlock also covers up to six people through Family Sharing.

My hints or streak freezes vanished after I reinstalled

If your device is signed into iCloud they should come back on their own: the game mirrors your progress and unspent balances into your iCloud storage, and reinstalling pulls them down. Give it a moment online on first launch. If they are still missing, write to us.

If you played without iCloud, the only copy was on your device. The game has no accounts and no servers of ours, so deleting it deleted that copy and unspent consumables cannot be recovered. We are sorry — it is the trade-off that comes with having nothing of yours on a server, and we say it in the terms before you buy.

I lost a streak on a day I definitely played

A day counts as done when the daily mosaic is finished during that calendar day in your device's local time. A missed day is covered automatically if you have a streak freeze banked. If you crossed time zones and think that broke a legitimate streak, send us the dates and roughly where you were, and we will look.

Do I need to be online?

No. The whole game plays offline. A connection is only needed to buy or restore a purchase, to post to Game Center leaderboards, and for the iCloud copy of your progress.

The leaderboards are not there

Leaderboards need Game Center. In iOS Settings, open Game Center and sign in. The game never requires it — the leaderboard buttons simply hide while you are signed out, and nothing else changes.

How do refunds work?

Apple processes every purchase and Apple decides refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We genuinely cannot issue one ourselves, but do tell us what went wrong.

The sound stopped

Check the toggles in the game's settings and the ring/silent switch first. If audio died after a phone call or an alarm, update to the latest version. If it still happens, write to us — that is a bug and we want it.

Accessibility

Smalti supports VoiceOver throughout, including placing and rotating tiles, along with Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion, a colour-blind assist mode and a left-handed layout. If any part of the game is unplayable with your setup, that is a bug rather than a limitation, and we would like to hear about it.
