The details

A mosaic arrives in pieces. You pull tesserae from a tray, rotate each one until it finds its cell, and the image assembles under your hands. When the last piece seats, the grout fuses, a sweep of glaze light crosses the surface, and the game holds the finished artwork still long enough to look at it.

That pause is the point. Nothing counts down at you, nothing interrupts, and there is no video ad waiting on the other side of a level. Smalti is built to be picked up for two minutes or forty, and to be exactly the same game either way.

Four hundred mosaics

The journey runs nine packs deep, from Workshop Basics through the Observatory to the Master's Atelier. Trays start at two pieces and grow to ten, and each pack introduces one new idea rather than a pile of them — irregular boards, colour-guided cells, pieces that set the instant you lay them, chiral pairs that deal together and bloom into symmetry when you fire them. Every level is solvable by construction, and none of them can be bought past.

One daily mosaic, the same for everyone

A fresh date-seeded mosaic each day, identical for every player. Keep a streak, bank streak freezes against the day life gets in the way, and share a result card that gives away no spoilers. Each day you solve fills its panel in that month's Monthly Mosaic; restore a full month to mint its seal.

Play it your way

  • Relax — untimed restoration at any difficulty, with a free allowance every day
  • Challenges — twenty commissions with time limits, move limits and no-hint runs, plus a fresh guest commission each week
  • Speedruns — five- and ten-mosaic sprints, and a weekly seeded run with Game Center leaderboards

Designed to be kind

  • Hints escalate gently and never dead-end a board
  • Stuck for a few attempts? Skip the level and come back to it later
  • Colour-blind assist, reduced motion, Dynamic Type, full dark mode, and playable end-to-end with VoiceOver
  • No ads. Not banners, not rewarded video, nothing to sit through

Honest premium

One purchase unlocks every pack, challenge and speedrun, permanently, with Family Sharing enabled. The free tier is a real game and not a demo: fifty-six journey levels across three packs, the daily mosaic, and relax restorations every day. Two higher patronage tiers exist for anyone who wants to fund the work — they unlock exactly the same content and buy no advantage.

No account, no sign-up, and the whole game works offline.
