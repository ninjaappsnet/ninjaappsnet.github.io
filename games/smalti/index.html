In play

The details

A mosaic arrives in pieces. You pull pieces from a tray, turn each one until it seats, and the picture assembles under your hands. When the last piece lands, the grout fuses, a sweep of glaze light crosses the surface, and the game holds the finished mosaic still long enough to look at it.

That pause is the point. Nothing counts down at you, nothing interrupts, and there is no video ad waiting on the other side of a level. Smalti is built to be picked up for two minutes or forty, and to be exactly the same game either way.

411 levels in 10 Rooms

The journey runs through 10 Rooms, from Workshop Basics through the Observatory to the Master's Atelier, plus a side Room, The Heirloom Set, that deals the same eight pieces on every level. In the main ascent the tray starts at two pieces and grows to ten, and each Room brings a new step rather than a pile of them — bigger boards with carved holes, colour-outlined cells that guide each new tray size, mirrored pieces, the Fold, that must be seated together as a pair. Every level is solvable by construction, and hints never finish a board for you.

One daily mosaic, the same for everyone

A fresh date-seeded mosaic each day, identical for every player. It is free and unmetered: a day is solved or it is not, with no cap on tries and no timer to beat. Keep a streak, replay any missed day from the calendar, and share a result card that gives away no spoilers. Each day you solve fills its panel in that month's Monthly Mosaic; restore every day of a month to seal it.

Play it your way

  • Relax — untimed restoration at any difficulty, with a free allowance every day
  • Daily Excavation — an ever-deeper ladder of mosaics, seeded fresh each day, with a free allowance of runs every day

Designed to be kind

  • Hints show a real placement from the solution and never dead-end a board
  • Stuck for a few tries? Skip the level and come back to it later
  • Colour-blind assist, reduced motion, Dynamic Type, a left-handed layout and full dark mode
  • No ads. Not banners, not rewarded video, nothing to sit through

One honest purchase

One purchase, the Unlock, opens every Room and the Commission that closes each one, permanently, with Family Sharing enabled. The free game is a real game and not a demo: 40 journey levels (the first two Rooms and the opening of Glass Hall), the daily mosaic, and a free allowance of Relax puzzles and Daily Excavation runs every day. Two higher supporter tiers exist for anyone who wants to fund the work — they unlock exactly the same content and buy no advantage.

No account, no sign-up, and the whole game works offline.
