The prompt bar works on every Mac and iPad that runs Diagrove. Without Apple Intelligence it can’t draw a diagram you describe in your own words, but it still reads steps, lists and most changes. When you click into the bar, it tells you Apple Intelligence is off and what still works.

## Draw a diagram

1. Open a new diagram (Command-N on the Mac, **New diagram** on iPad) and click the prompt bar.
2. Type the steps in one of these forms, then press Return (on iPad, tap the arrow):
   - Arrows: `Idea -> Plan -> Build -> Ship`. A step ending in a question mark, such as `Paid?`, becomes a decision. Label a line with `Paid? -[Yes]-> Send receipt` or `Paid? -> Send receipt: Yes`.
   - Several lines of arrows, pasted in, make one diagram: a step with the same name is the same shape.
   - A sentence: “Sign up, confirm your email, then log in” becomes three steps, one after another.
   - A list pasted in, one topic per line, indented under its parent, becomes a tree of topics.
3. Diagrove draws boxes and arrows and lays them out. The notice’s **Undo** takes them away.

## Change a diagram

These changes are read without Apple Intelligence too. Each one shows on the canvas first: **Apply** makes it one undo step, **Discard** puts it back.

- By name: “make all decisions orange”, “rename Cart to Basket”, “add a step after Pay called Fraud check”, “connect Try again to Cart”, “move Try again below Send receipt”, “change 2% to 3%”, “delete the notes”.
- On what you’ve selected: “make it orange”, “make them 50% wider”, “align them left”, “group them”.
- The layout: “tidy up the whole diagram”.

The sparkle at the start of the bar (or **Help › What you can ask the prompt bar**) lists what you can ask. Without Apple Intelligence it leaves out the examples that need it.

## What needs Apple Intelligence

Describing a diagram in your own words, such as “Draw a flowchart for returning an order”. Either way, the bar draws boxes and arrows; for posters, infographics or charts, start from a [template](../../guide/templates/) or [stencils](../../guide/stencils-and-tints/). Everything runs on your device, and nothing you type is sent anywhere.
