Diagrove reads a hand-drawn diagram and draws it again as shapes, connected lines and their words, ready to edit. On iPad you sketch straight onto the canvas; on the Mac you give it a picture. The reading happens on your device.

## On iPad: Sketch mode

1. Open a diagram and tap **Sketch** in the toolbar.
2. Draw boxes, circles, diamonds and the arrows between them with Apple Pencil, or with your finger. Write the words inside the shapes and on the arrows.
3. Use **Eraser** to rub out a stroke, or **Clear** to start again. Undo works on the ink while you sketch.
4. Tap **Turn into diagram**. The shapes and lines appear where you drew them, selected, as one step you can undo; the ink goes and Sketch mode ends. To have them arranged for you instead, tap **Tidy** in the notice that follows.
5. To leave Sketch mode without reading the ink, tap **Done**. The ink waits for you while the window is open.

Once you’ve used Apple Pencil, only the Pencil draws and a finger scrolls, as in Notes. The ink isn’t saved with the Document.

## On the Mac: a sketch or photo

1. Choose **File › New diagram from sketch or photo…** for a new Document, or **Insert › Diagram from sketch or photo…** to add to the canvas you’re on.
2. Choose a photo of a whiteboard or a paper sketch, or a screenshot. You can also take one with your iPhone through Continuity Camera, or drop a picture on the sheet.
3. The sheet shows your picture with what it found drawn over it, and the diagram it will insert beside it. Anything it’s unsure of is dashed and named in a note.
4. Choose **Keep my layout** for shapes where you drew them, lined up on the grid, or **Tidy** to have them arranged for you.
5. Click **Insert**.

A picture already on the canvas can be read too: select it and choose **Turn into diagram…** from its context menu or the inspector’s Picture section.

![The Turn your sketch into a diagram sheet: the whiteboard photo on the left with the shapes it found outlined, the diagram on the right with 4 shapes, 3 lines and 5 labels, Keep my layout chosen, and Insert.](sketch-sheet.webp "The found shapes outlined over the photo, and the diagram beside it.")

## For the best reading

Draw closed outlines with dark lines on light paper, take the photo straight on with the whole drawing in view, and keep the words inside their shapes or beside their lines. Diagrove reads rectangles, rounded rectangles, ellipses, diamonds, triangles, hexagons, parallelograms and cylinders.
