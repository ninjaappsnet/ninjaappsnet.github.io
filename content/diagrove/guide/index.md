Diagrove draws precise diagrams on your Mac and iPad, and then makes them do something. Each page of the guide takes one part of the app and says what it’s for and how to use it, with the differences between the Mac and the iPad noted where they come up. If you’d rather learn by building something, start with the [tutorials](../tutorials/); for one task at a time, the [how-to pages](../how-to/); and for questions about the app itself, the [support page](../support.html).
